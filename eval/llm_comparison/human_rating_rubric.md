# Blind human-rating rubric

Rate each output from 1 to 5. Judge only the shown prompt/context and response.

## Language quality

1. Hard to understand or uses the wrong language/script.
2. Many language or phrasing problems.
3. Understandable, with noticeable issues.
4. Clear and natural, with minor issues.
5. Clear, natural, and appropriate to the requested language/style.

## Usefulness

1. Does not answer or is misleading.
2. Mostly unhelpful or misses key instructions.
3. Partly useful, with important gaps.
4. Useful and follows the request, with minor gaps.
5. Directly useful, grounded, and follows the request.

If uncertain about a Tunisian Arabic or Arabizi phrasing, note that in
`comments` rather than guessing.
